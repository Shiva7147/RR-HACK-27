Algorithmic Formulation
Mathematical definition of pressure.

Evaluating 80,498 deliveries through a dynamic stress vector to eliminate flat-track noise and quantify true execution under championship leverage.

1. Dynamic Pressure Weight Formulation & Neutral Baseline Defaults

Every delivery (i) in the historical database is mapped to a continuous pressure coefficient W_i in [1.00, 2.25]:

W_i = 1.00 + (c_phase * I_phase) + (c_rrr * I_rrr) + (c_wkt * I_wkt) + (c_margin * I_margin)

Baseline Default: When no stress triggers are active (e.g. overs 7–14 with required run rate < 8.0 and wickets intact), W_i = 1.00. The weight scales dynamically up to 2.25 during high-leverage death chases, wicket clusters, and razor-margin finishes.

2. Relative Clutch Index (RCI) for Batting and Bowling
Batting Relative Clutch Index (RCI_Bat)
RCI_Bat = SR_Press * [1 + (SR_Press - SR_Base)/SR_Base] * (1 - DotRate_Press) * (1 + BndRate_Press)

Penalizes batters who inflate statistics during low-pressure phases but accumulate dot balls when W_i ≥ 1.40. Identifies high-strike-rate, low-dot-rate finishers.

Bowling Relative Clutch Index (RCI_Bowl)
RCI_Bowl = (100 / Econ_Press) * [1 + (Econ_Base - Econ_Press)/Econ_Base] * DotRate_Press * (1 - BndRate_Press)

Measures boundary suppression and dot-ball creation under extreme leverage. Directly filters for seamers who maintain composure under dew.
